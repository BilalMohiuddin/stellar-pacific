Accessibility Statement

Our Commitment

Stellar Pacific is committed to making our website accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. We strive to ensure that our website meets or exceeds the requirements of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Accessibility Features

We have taken the following measures to ensure accessibility on our website:

- Alternative text descriptions for images
- Sufficient color contrast between text and background
- Keyboard-navigable interface
- Reduced motion options for users who prefer minimal animation
- Semantic HTML structure for screen reader compatibility
- Responsive design for various screen sizes and devices

Third-Party Content

While we strive to ensure accessibility across our entire website, some third-party content or features may not be fully accessible. We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of all content.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us.
